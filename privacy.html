Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

This policy covers this website and Royal Path Uploader, the private tool The Royal Path uses to upload and schedule videos on its own YouTube channel.

Who uses the uploader

Only the owner of The Royal Path YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in with Google, it is granted access to our own YouTube channel only, so that it can upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and add them to our playlists. It does not access, collect or store any other person’s data, comments, or viewing information.

What is stored, and where

The Google sign-in token and the IDs of videos we have just uploaded are kept only on the channel owner’s own computer. Nothing is sent to any server of ours, sold or shared with anyone. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account security page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token from the owner’s computer also removes it.

YouTube and Google

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
