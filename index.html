The Royal Path is a YouTube channel that makes short, carefully sourced videos about the Catholic faith: a saint for each feast day, prayers asking the saints’ intercession, and teaching series such as The Creed, line by line, which explains why Catholics say each phrase of the Nicene Creed.

Every teaching point in our videos is tied to Scripture or the Catechism of the Catholic Church, and the sources are listed in each video’s description.
